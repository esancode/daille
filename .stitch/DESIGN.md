# Velune Pratas Strict Visual System

## 6. Design System Notes for Stitch Generation
**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- **CRITICAL AESTHETIC:** Strictly replicate the provided high-contrast reference layout. Minimalist, modern luxury, editorial style.
- **Colors:** STRICTLY Black (#000000) and White (#FFFFFF). NO primary colors, NO secondary accents. Pure high-contrast monochrome. Use very light gray (#F5F5F5) only for subtle backgrounds if absolutely necessary.
- **Buttons:** Sharp edges! NO rounded corners. Solid black rectangles with white text, or solid white with black text and black borders. Text inside buttons must be bold, uppercase.
- **Typography:**
  - Headers/Display: Large, bold, clean sans-serif (e.g. Montserrat/Inter) or modern geometric serif. High impact, high contrast.
  - Body/Labels: Clean, highly legible sans-serif. Use ALL CAPS for labels, buttons, and subheadings.
- **Layout Elements:**
  - Solid black full-width bars for separating sections or for the footer.
  - Product images must be shown in black boxes or on clean white backgrounds.
  - Lifestyle imagery should be high-contrast, black and white, or very desaturated.
  - Generous whitespace, clean lines, no shadows, no gradients. Flat, stark, premium editorial feel.
