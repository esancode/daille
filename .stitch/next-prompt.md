A striking, editorial Customer Dashboard page for a high-end silver jewelry store. STRICTLY follow the high-contrast, black-and-white visual style of the reference layout.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- **CRITICAL AESTHETIC:** Strictly replicate the provided high-contrast reference layout. Minimalist, modern luxury, editorial style.
- **Colors:** STRICTLY Black (#000000) and White (#FFFFFF). NO primary colors, NO secondary accents. Pure high-contrast monochrome. Use very light gray (#F5F5F5) only for subtle backgrounds if absolutely necessary.
- **Buttons:** Sharp edges! NO rounded corners. Solid black rectangles with white text, or solid white with black text and black borders. Text inside buttons must be bold, uppercase.
- **Typography:**
  - Headers/Display: Large, bold, clean sans-serif (e.g. Montserrat/Inter) or modern geometric serif. High impact, high contrast.
  - Body/Labels: Clean, highly legible sans-serif. Use ALL CAPS for labels, buttons, and subheadings.
- **Layout Elements:**
  - Solid black full-width bars for separating sections or for the footer.
  - Generous whitespace, clean lines, no shadows, no gradients. Flat, stark, premium editorial feel.

**Page Structure:**
1. **Header:** Clean white navigation bar. Left: "VELUNE PRATAS". Center: Menu items ("ANÉIS", "COLARES", "BRINCOS"). Right: Search, Profile, Cart.
2. **Dashboard Layout:** A 2-column layout for the customer area.
    - **Left Sidebar:** A minimalist vertical menu with sharp active state (black background, white text). Items: "MEUS PEDIDOS", "MEUS DADOS", "ENDEREÇOS", "SAIR".
    - **Right Content Area:**
      - **Welcome Banner:** Large bold text "BEM-VINDA, MARIA!".
      - **Order History:** A clean, minimal list of past orders. Each order shows Order #, Date, Status (e.g. "ENTREGUE"), and a sharp black "VER DETALHES" button.
      - **Personal Data Snippet:** A crisp white card with black border showing name, email, and a button "EDITAR DADOS".
3. **Footer:** Solid black full-width footer. Centered white logo "VELUNE PRATAS". A solid black button with white border "INSTAGRAM". White footer links and copyright.
